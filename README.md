Make the AI judge feel curious. Your program writes one text (a short text written to intrigue the AI judge reading it), an AI judge compares the texts two at a time, and the 20 best win rates share $50. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Make the AI judge feel curious" bounty, $50 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
npx tsx main.ts
```

The program writes a single short text to `/output/entry.txt`: a door behind the atlas shelf that no one remembers building, and a cup of tea that is empty every morning.
